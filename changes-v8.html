Sic Semper, Version 8: what changed

Compared with Version 7. 4 paragraphs changed. Old text is on top with removed words; new text below with added words.

Questions for you (plot, clue, motive)

No plot, clue or motive changes. Calendar and payment wording only, to match CQD's calendar (autumn 2026 to spring 2027):
- Ch 2, 21, 31: "a hundred and sixty years" is now "more than a hundred and sixty years" (1865 to 2026-27 is 161 to 162 years).
- Ch 28: "double again" is now "six thousand again", so it doesn't read as a second increase to twelve thousand.
Audio note: the Listen narration for ch 2, 21 and 31 still says the old wording until those chapters are narrated again.

I looked at him. He could not have been much past seventy-five. The inauguration he was describing had happened more than a hundred and sixty years ago.

Chapter 21: Tenth Street 1 changed

I have heard that scream in my sleep for more than a hundred and sixty years.

Chapter 28: The Arsenal 1 changed

It was the fifteenth visit. The payment for the last session had come on Monday, six thousand again, with the same note, and I had stopped being surprised by it and started being uneasy. I had not said so to him. I thought he knew.

I read the last line again. My eyes had filled, and I held the letter out over the table, away from me, until they cleared. It was more than a hundred and sixty years old, and I was not going to be the one who put a mark on it.
